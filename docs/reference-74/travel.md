# Travel in 7.4

Sources: each travel NPC's TibiaWiki page as it stood before 7.5 (released 2005-08-09), or its earliest revision
after that (the late-2005 pages already list Port Hope, which came with 7.5 - left out). Liberty Bay (7.8) and
Svargrond (8.0) are later still. Every ship and carpet is premium only; the Ice Island ferries are free to use. A Grand
Postman pays 10 gp less a passage (the pages: "Completing the Postman Quest will reduce the ship fare by 10 gp";
npc/lib/postman.lua).
